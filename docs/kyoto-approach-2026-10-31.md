## Defined approach scope and unresolved connections

- **Gion research segment:** the public-street approach on Shijo-dori from the east end of Shijo-Ohashi to the Shijo-dori/Hanamikoji junction. This bounded extent is a research/acceptance choice, not a newly surveyed route or a declaration that every adjoining street is public. The city area guide supplies the district context; the station map locates Exit 7. The connection between the lift, crossings and this segment still needs checking. Do not expand this into private lanes or an entire Gion circuit.
- **Museum station connection:** [Getting Here](https://www.kyohaku.go.jp/eng/visit/access/) directs visitors east along Shichijo Street for about seven minutes from Keihan Shichijo. The [September 1 Keihan station map](https://www.keihan.co.jp/traffic/station/assets/pdf/info/station-160.pdf) was visually inspected, but the reviewed museum instructions do not select a numbered station exit. No exit-to-South-Gate continuous step-free route is published here.
- **Kiyomizu ordinary visit:** the temple FAQ/access pages did not establish the ordinary adult/child price or a general no-reservation policy. A different Kiyomizu temple, special-garden fee, past charity event or third-party price is not substituted. The [2026 night-viewing page](https://www.kiyomizudera.or.jp/event/yakan.php) confirms only its announced event scope; the existing booking text, October 30 review date and November 30 expiry are retained rather than being generalized to ordinary visits.
- **Fushimi lower grounds:** the shrine's [map](https://inari.jp/en/map/) retains Romon as a lower-ground landmark. [JR's Inari station map page](https://eki.jr-odekake.net/premises?id=0621613) identifies a layout dated February 17, 2020; it is not current physical evidence of the shrine approach. Station directions or a landmark do not establish an accessible gate or mountain route.

Fushimi's [English FAQ](https://inari.jp/en/faq/), [Japanese FAQ](https://inari.jp/about/faq/), map, access and [prayer-service page](https://inari.jp/grace/gokito/) were read. **Hours, admission, booking and accessibility remain Unknown.** The prayer-service schedule and offering amounts do not establish general entry hours, ordinary admission, reservations or a continuous accessible path. No 24-hour, free-entry or step-free claim is inferred, and those four fields receive no new review date.
