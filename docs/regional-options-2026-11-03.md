## Niigata: Marinepia Nihonkai

- Proposed canonical ID: `pref:niigata:marinepia-nihonkai` (new ID, subject to integration review).
- Names: ja `新潟市水族館 マリンピア日本海`; en `Niigata City Aquarium Marinepia Nihonkai`; th `พิพิธภัณฑ์สัตว์น้ำเมืองนีงาตะ มารีนเปีย นิฮงไค` (Thai translation proposed, not independently reviewed).
- Address: `新潟県新潟市中央区西船見町5932-445`, 951-8555. Official facility coordinates, latitude/longitude: `[37.9234877, 139.0277849]` from the [Niigata City aquarium GIS dataset](https://www.city.niigata.lg.jp/shisei/seisaku/it/open-data/opendata-gis/od-gis_kankobunspo/od-gis_suizokukan.html), [CSV](https://www.city.niigata.lg.jp/shisei/seisaku/it/open-data/opendata-gis/od-gis_kankobunspo/od-gis_suizokukan.files/od_gis_10114_suizokukan.csv). Dataset is JGD2000 and last updated 2022-04-01; not an entrance survey.
- The [operator access page](https://www.marinepia.or.jp/access/) gives the address and JR Niigata Station terminal platform 9, C22 to the `水族館前` terminus, approximately 25 minutes. Check current departures; service may change on busy dates. The [operator's English guide](https://www.marinepia.or.jp/mlg/en.html) lists 20 minutes, so use the Japanese access page's approximate figure and do not present it as a guaranteed journey time.
- [Operator visitor information](https://www.marinepia.or.jp/info/): normally 09:00–17:00, ticket sales until 16:30; closed December 29–January 1 and the first Thursday of March plus the following day. Schedules can change during busy periods. [English operator guide](https://www.marinepia.or.jp/mlg/en.html): adults ¥1,500, elementary/junior-high students ¥600, ages 4–5 ¥200, ages 3 and under free; verify current fees before travel.
- Scope: indoor aquarium displays plus an outdoor Niigata Field; do not describe the entire site as indoors. No station-to-venue step-free route or entrance accessibility is asserted here. The operator links a wheelchair/stroller route map on the visitor information page; review that source separately before adding accessibility claims. Booking remains unverified for individual visits.
- Alternative considered: [Niigata City History Museum](https://www.city.niigata.lg.jp/chuo/shisetsu/yoka/bunka/minatopia.html) main building closed from 2026-09-01 until approximately the end of June 2027 for HVAC work, subject to change. The former customs house remains open; do not market the main museum as a currently open indoor alternative.

## Toyama: Toyama Glass Art Museum

- Proposed canonical ID: `pref:toyama:toyama-glass-art-museum` (new ID, subject to integration review).
- Names: ja `富山市ガラス美術館`; en `Toyama Glass Art Museum`; th `พิพิธภัณฑ์ศิลปะแก้วเมืองโทยามะ` (Thai translation proposed, not independently reviewed).
- Address: `富山県富山市西町5番1号 TOYAMAキラリ`, 930-0062. Latitude/longitude `[36.68855, 137.215052]` from the municipal [public Wi-Fi open dataset](https://opdt.city.toyama.lg.jp/dataset/wirelesslan), [CSV](https://opdt.city.toyama.lg.jp/dataset/f9b8cc06-aba0-4d27-aefc-347c50e62726/resource/f40cb5a3-9b1c-43ee-bc16-5f0bb5d94453/download/162019_public_wireless_lan.csv), row `富山市ガラス美術館` / `TOYAMAキラリ内`, Wi-Fi service area 1F lobby. JGD2011; dataset dated March 2025. This is a facility reference point, not a verified entrance.
- [Operator visitor information](https://toyama-glass-art-museum.jp/visitor/): from Toyama Station, the circular tram takes approximately 12 minutes to Grand Plaza-mae, then about 2 minutes on foot; the Minami-Toyama-ekimae tram takes approximately 12 minutes to Nishimachi, then about 1 minute on foot. Confirm current service and the final walking route.
- The same operator page lists permanent and special exhibitions 09:30–18:00, last entry 30 minutes before closing; usually closed first and third Wednesdays and New Year. Exhibition changes and individual special exhibitions may alter closures. The permanent exhibition is ¥200 for adults/university students; special exhibitions have separate fees. Check the [operator calendar](https://toyama-glass-art-museum.jp/) before visiting. An older [municipal Culture Day page](https://www.city.toyama.lg.jp/bunka/bunka/1010543/1013512.html) mentions Friday/Saturday evening hours; the operator's current visitor page says 18:00, so do not carry the longer hours forward without confirmation.
- Scope: indoor museum in the shared TOYAMAキラリ building. No step-free station route or entrance claim is established by the sources above. The operator asks groups of 20 or more to contact it in advance; individual reservation requirements have not been verified. Do not describe tickets or access as uniformly applicable to all exhibitions.
